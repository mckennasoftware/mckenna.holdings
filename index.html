What we do

Each product is owned and operated under the company, funded by its own work rather than outside capital. That keeps the roadmap answerable to the people who actually use it.

Narrow by design

One product, one job. We would rather finish a small thing properly than ship a broad thing that never quite works.

Independently held

No investors and no acquisition clock. Products are kept, maintained, and improved for as long as people use them.

Built to hold

The company exists to own the work. As a product grows, it can stand on its own as a subsidiary without disrupting anything already running.
